What the analysis says

Default is rare and uneven: external credit scores, occupation, education and age separate good from risky clients far better than loan size or income. A tuned LightGBM model with six financial ratios beats a logistic baseline by +0.022 AUC, and the gain is statistically clear. Its scores are calibrated to real probabilities, so they can drive a cost-based approve / review / decline policy.
